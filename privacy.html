Our Privacy Commitment

KLONDIKE is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy is simple: we don't collect, store, or share your personal data.

What We Don't Collect

We do not collect, store, or transmit:

  • Personal information (name, email, phone number, etc.)
  • Location data
  • Usage analytics or tracking data
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Any information that can identify you

Crash Reports

The only data we collect is automatic crash reports to help us fix bugs and improve app stability. These reports contain technical information about the crash but do not include any personal information.

Local Storage

Your game progress and settings are stored locally on your device only. This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

No Third-Party Sharing

We do not share any information with third parties because we don't collect any information to share.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

If we ever change our data collection practices, we will update this policy and notify users within the app. The "Last Updated" date at the top will reflect any changes.
